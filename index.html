📋 Company Profile

TRUSTED PARTNER IN GLOBAL TRADE

Ash Haven Global Ltd is a duly registered Ghanaian company authorized to carry on business in import, export, supply of general goods, and mining. Operating from East Legon, Accra, we serve clients across Ghana and beyond.

Our commitment is to deliver reliable, transparent, and efficient trade solutions — building lasting partnerships grounded in integrity and professionalism.

  • Fully licensed and registered in Ghana
  • Expertise across import, export & mining sectors
  • End-to-end logistics and documentation support
